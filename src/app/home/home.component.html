Who will get benefited?

Patients, doctors and helpers will benefit from this system.

Patient

A patient needs to register into the system first with the required information to be able to use different functionalities of the system.


  • - Upload previous medical history
  • - Schedule doctor's appointment
  • - Make online payment
  • - 1-to-1 video consultation with doctor
  • - Get prescription online

Click here to register as a patient.

Doctor

A Doctor needs to have a valid registration number (from any state medical council) to register and practise through this system.


  • - Register as doctor and wait for the system admin to validate.
  • - Once validated, doctor will be able to login and create appointments.
  • - Get data of those (patients) who booked the above created appointment.
  • - Video consult online.
  • - Connect to EHR to generate patient's prescription.

Click here to register as a doctor.

General Helper

Anyone with some general understanding of the system can be a helper. A helper is there to help the patients throughout the entire process from account verification, medical record upload to scheduling doctor's appointment and making payment.


  • - Register as a helper.
  • - Admin after validating the data, will map you with patients.
  • - Help patients, with data upload, scheduling appointment, online payment etc.
  • - Help patients with verification also, if required.

Click here to register as a helper.

Helper - Medical Student

A medical student can also register as a helper by selecting "Helper Type" as "Medical Student".


  • - Select "Helper Type" : "Medical Intern" during registration.
  • - Once registered, wait for the admin validation.
  • - On successful validation, you will be able to help patients with selecting doctors based on the symptoms.
  • - Can perform all the tasks, a general helper can perform.

Click here to register as a helper.
