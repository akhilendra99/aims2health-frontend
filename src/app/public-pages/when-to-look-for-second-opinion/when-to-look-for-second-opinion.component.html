When to look for 2nd opinion?

Per 2nd Opinion Case:
  • Treatment taken but symptoms persist
  • Diagnosis unclear, experimental treatment, diagnosed with a rare disease, or no consensus how to treat a complex case
  • Treatment is costly, risky, toxic or has lifelong impact
  • Reconfirm cancer diagnosis
  • Advised to not take any treatment for widespread cancer
  • Uncertain about utility of surgical option
  • Earlier surgery led to unexpected problems/complications
  • Unsure about utility of expensive surgical or medical treatment
  • Unsure whether to apply enhanced recovery after surgery protocol instead of conventional perioperative care to reduce postoperative stress, increases patient satisfaction, and reduces postoperative stay and cost
  • When you're considering participating in a trial for a new drug
  • Suggested a new or experimental approach/procedure involving experimental instruments/devices
  • Suggested option requires travel to a distant city or country;
  • Difficulty choice amongst multiple suitable options
