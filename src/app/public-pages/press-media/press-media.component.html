May 24th, 2021- India Fights Back : Telemedicine a boon during pandemic

In this episode of India Fights Back (IFB) based on Telemedicine a boon during pandemic. The Telemedicine Practice Guidelines were released jointly by the Ministry of Health and Family welfare and NITI Aayog ensures access to medical advice does not become challenging due to social distancing norms mandated by COVID-19 outbreak. The Guidelines, coupled with the Government’s tele-consultation services, e-Sanjeevani and e-Sanjeevani OPD, have leveraged information communication technologies to enable diagnosis, treatment and management of diseases. National Teleconsultation Service of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare is first of its kind onlineOPD service offered by a country’s government to its citizens.
