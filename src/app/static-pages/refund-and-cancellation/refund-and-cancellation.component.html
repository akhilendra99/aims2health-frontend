Refund & Cancellations

 VirtualSathi Patient refund and cancellation policy.

a. If you have not paid anything to VirtualSathi:
i. The question of refund doesn’t arise.
ii. We start processing a case only after the payment of at least Rs. 500 (for a single specialist 2nd opinion) has been received by VirtualSathi.
iii. We may, however, still keep your information for an year in our system; that is, in the patient portal which you can access. This would be useful in case you need our services at a later date.
b. If you have made the full payment of Rs. 2,500 (Rs. 3,500 for two specialists or Rs. 5,000 for three or more specialists in an appropriately constituted Multispecialty Board, as the case may be) to VirtualSathi for consultation with a Specialist/Board:
i. Please recall that the sum paid by you includes a non-refundable basic registration fee of Rs. 200. However, even this sum would be eligible for a refund in the following situations:
1. If patient dies within 24 hours of your having made the payment. Share death certificate within the next 30 days to obtain a complete refund including these Rs. 200.
2. If VirtualSathi has not started processing your case within the next working day after the date on which you made the payment.
3. If you are dissatisfied with the quality of our service.
ii. Please note that the sum paid by you also includes a referral note preparation (processing) fee of Rs. 300 by VirtualSathi. This processing is essential to make the best use of the time of the specialist (or Board) to be consulted.
1. If VirtualSathi has not taken any steps to process your case latest by the end of the first two working hour on the next working date following the date on which you made the payment, then this sum of Rs. 300 is also eligible for a refund.
2. If VirtualSathi has started processing your case (in the timeframe indicated in point a. above) to prepare a self-contained referral summary note then this sum of Rs. 300 is not eligible for a refund. However, if you are eventually dissatisfied with the quality of the self-contained referral summary note, we will refund the Rs. 300 as well.
3. 50% refund of the fee payable to the Specialist/Board:
a. If you cancel your first phone or video-consultation appointment with a Specialist/Board in the first four (4) working hours of making the payment, we will refund 50% of the fee payable to the Specialist/Board.
b. If the first phone or video consultation appointment was cancelled Specialist/Board due to the death or emergency hospitalization of the patient.
4. If you cancel your first phone or video-consultation appointment with a Specialist/Board after four (4) working hours of making the payment you will be eligible for no refund of the fee payable to the Specialist/Board irrespective of when your first phone or video-consultation appointment with a specialist was scheduled.
5. Phone or video-consultation appointment may be rescheduled a maximum of 2 times at no extra cost: If you make a written request four (4) hours prior to the phone or video-consultation appointment to reschedule it, this would be done at no extra cost to the patient team. This can be done once more at no extra cost to the patient team. Any further rescheduling will incur 50% additional fee payable to the Specialist/Board, as the case may be.
6. If due to a network connectivity issue of the VirtualSathi Server you are unable to complete the first phone or video consultation then VirtualSathi support will book a new video consultation session without levying any additional cost. The rescheduled session(s) may be on the same day or on the next working day (or later if you so wish) but exact scheduling would also depend on availability of the Specialist/Board. Alternatively, you will be eligible for a full refund of the fee payable to the Specialist/Board.
7. No show at a phone or video consultation appointment with a Specialist/Board. No refund even if the appointments was cancelled due to a network connectivity issues on your side, unless you formally seek a rescheduling of it during the scheduled time of the phone or video-consultation. An appointment can be so rescheduled a maximum of two (2) times at no extra cost to you. Any further rescheduling will incur 50% additional fee payable to the Specialist/Board, as the case may be.
iii. If VirtualSathi is unable to provide a Specialist/Board for consultation as per your request within the next five working day after you have made the payment, you have the right to ask for a full refund, except Rs. 500 (which includes the Rs. 200 basic registration fee and fee of Rs. 300 for preparation of the self-contained referral summary note. At the same time, VirtualSathi shall provide you a copy of a self-contained referral summary note for your use elsewhere.
iv. Where the Specialist/Board has completed the 2nd Opinion consultation and the written report has not yet been shared with Patient Team: No refund
v. Where the Specialist/Board has completed the 2nd Opinion consultation and the written report has been shared with Patient Team: No refund unless issues of quality of the report is raised in writing/digitally within 15 days of the receipt of the 2nd opinion report by the Patient Team. The comments on quality of the report must be specific, clear and need not be technical. Satisfaction of the patient team is our absolute priority. Make a request/grievance/complaint to Director (Customer Care), Director (Administration) or the CMD of VirtualSathi (preferably in that order) and if the management of VirtualSathi find the balance of convenience in your favour, VirtualSathi will make a full refund including the Rs. 500 charged for basic registration fee plus the referral note preparation (processing) fee.
vi. For any delay in refunding the money for reasons beyond the control of VirtualSathi, no extra amount would be payable as compensation for the unforeseen and justifiable delay.
vii. For any situation where you are dissatisfied, whether that situation is covered or not covered by the above listed points, you have the right to take up the matter in writing/digitally, as a request/grievance/complaint with Director (Customer Care), Director (Administration) or the CMD of VirtualSathi. Normally, a written/digital response to your request/grievance/complaint from customer care of VirtualSathi. The written decision of the management of VirtualSathi in this regard would be final.
Refund Process (if applicable)
c. To complete your refund, please contact VirtualSathi using the same booking reference number and a covering Refund Demand Letter (Copy Enclosed) to which you attach all relevant documents such as a death certificate or an emergency hospitalization report.
d. Once your cancellation/refund request is received, VirtualSathi will notify you by email or SMS of having received it. It will be reviewed expeditiously as per this policy. Once a decision is taken, VirtualSathi notify you of the approval or rejection of your request for refund and the reason(s) for it. If you refund request is approved, your refund will be processed, and would be credited automatically to your credit card or original method of payment, within 15 working days.
e. You may review the refund status in the appropriate tab of the Patient team portal on the VirtualSathi website.
Late or missing refunds (if applicable) If you haven’t received a refund within 30 days of making a written/digital request/ grievance/ complaint, or 15 days of hearing from VirtualSathi that a refund has been made, first recheck your credit card account as it may take some time before the refund process is completed. Next contact your bank as there may be a processing delay in the bank too. If you have done all of this and you still have not received the full or partial refund, please contact us at secy@virtualsathi.in.
